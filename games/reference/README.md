# games/reference

The reference game: a Vite app plus `sim/`, a game crate on the engine's `Game` trait. Working notes: `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Pattern A (default):** write no worker code; `createClient` builds its own workers. **Pattern B:** a two-line `src/worker.ts` (`import { run } from 'engine/worker'; run()`) and `createClient({ createWorker: () => new Worker(new URL('./worker.ts', import.meta.url), { type: 'module' }) })`: the fix for a project with no workspace marker above it and for non-Vite bundlers (0017 §3).
- **Support check:** `const support = await checkSupport()` before starting. `support.ok` is false when `failures` is non-empty; branch on each failure's `code` (`not-isolated`, `no-sab`, `no-wasm`, `no-module-worker`, `no-webgpu`, `no-adapter`, `limits-too-low`), never on `message` (developer English).
- **Warnings** (`support.warnings`: `no-opfs`, `no-web-locks`) mean the game runs with less (a world is `durable: false`, no cross-tab lock); show them or ignore them, they never stop it.
- **Capability screen:** `src/ui/capability.ts` shows one line per failure instead of a blank canvas (`main.ts` calls it first); it keeps `data-code` on each line.

## The game

A deliberately small game that exercises every engine feature, single-player and multiplayer, in its own private package. Scope is exactly what follows; do not add to it. Tyler owns these requirements. Seconds are authored and stored as ticks (20 Hz; [0006](../../docs/decisions/0006-time-units.md)). Dated lines are Tyler's answers.

### World

- Infinite, seeded, deterministic ([`world-and-worldgen.md`](../../docs/architecture/world-and-worldgen.md)). `RefWorldgen` runs a 5-octave height and a 3-octave moisture simplex fBm (`sim/src/worldgen.rs`) into five base tiles: deep water, water, sand, grass, dirt.
- Resources are iron, wood, stone and coal, scattered per tile by `hash2`: wood only on grass, stone only on dirt and sand, iron and coal on grass, dirt and sand, none on water. A resource is a layer over the base tile, not a tile kind. Each holds 10 units (`aux`), one per collect, and vanishes at 0.
- Look: 16 px tiles, 4 variants per terrain, pre-generated by `scripts/gen-assets.mjs` into `assets/`. The per-tile brightness jitter and edge dithering come from the engine's shader, not from the variants (which are flat).
- Placement asks the tiles, never a tile type: water and furnaces carry `NOT_BUILDABLE` ([0007](../../docs/decisions/0007-world-model.md) §6).

### Players

- Drawn as circles (own player: circle plus a faint range ring; remote players in their palette colour). The player follows its own camera with a critically damped spring (`client.rs`, runs per client).
- **Position is presence, not world state** ([0001](../../docs/decisions/0001-camera-and-presence.md)): relayed unlogged to other clients. A collect carries the claimed position; `admit` rejects a claim over 16 tiles from the host's last sample and `apply` range-checks 3 tiles.
- Within 3 tiles (player centre to tile centre) of a resource, a collect button appears over it (one per resource in range). Collecting takes 2 s and the button fills. One collect and one craft at a time, no queue (`Busy`). Panning out of range sends `CancelCollect`. Two players may collect one tile (not a reservation); a tile that is gone at completion yields nothing. A disconnect cancels a collect; a craft keeps running.
- Inventory (stone, iron, wood, coal, furnace, ingot) and unlocks are per player. A new player spawns at the land tile nearest the origin; a returning one resumes where it was. Players may float over water.
- A roster of coloured dots (`Ui.roster`, 8-colour palette assigned on join) shows who is connected.

### Crafting and building

- After mining 5 stone the furnace recipe unlocks and the crafting menu appears. A furnace costs 5 stone (paid at start) and takes 5 s.
- A crafted furnace is placed from construction mode: 2x2 tiles, not on water or another furnace. It may cover resource tiles; a covered resource cannot be collected until the furnace is picked up (Tyler, 2026-10-10, R1). Mouse: the ghost follows the cursor and a click places. Touch: a tap moves the ghost and a DOM Confirm button places.

### Furnace

- Clicking a furnace opens its panel: deposit iron, and coal or wood as fuel (slot cap 999). One smelt takes 5 s and makes one ingot; one coal fuels 10 ingots, one wood 2 (coal burns first). Fuel is lit at smelt start, ore consumed at smelt end.
- Any player can use any furnace. "Take all" moves the ingots out; ore and fuel stay in. Taking is predicted like every other action (Tyler, 2026-10-10, R2).
- An empty furnace (no ore, fuel, lit fuel or ingots) can be picked up by any player: it despawns and returns to that player's inventory. (Added by Tyler, 2026-09-19, so the game exercises entity removal.)

### UI

- Framework-free TypeScript (`src/ui/`); the engine does not care.
- "Export world" is always offered in the game UI, not only on the save-incompatible screen (Tyler, 2026-10-10, R4; `src/ui/export.ts`).

### Not implemented

None: every requirement above is met by the code.

## Hosting

Cross-origin isolation (0015 §3) needs **two headers on every response, 404s and worker scripts included**, with exactly these values:

```
Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin
Cross-Origin-Embedder-Policy: require-corp
```

`engine/vite` sets them in dev and preview. `node scripts/check-coi.mjs <url>` asserts both, exact values, on `/`, the hashed worker script, the `.wasm`, an image and a 404. Limits (0015 Consequences): no GitHub Pages (it cannot set headers), no CORP-less third-party content, no popup-based OAuth; assets are same-origin; a phone-hosted single-player world must fit the sim arena.

**Verified:** our own handler, `games/reference-server --static <dir>` on a Fly machine ([`../reference-server/README.md`](../reference-server/README.md)), and **Cloudflare Pages** with the server on another origin ([0072](../../docs/decisions/0072-static-host-verified-build-time-server-url.md)): `check-coi.mjs` passes against both and the page reaches `online`.

**A static deploy:** `VITE_SERVER_URL=wss://<server>/ws pnpm --filter reference build`, then upload `dist/`. The build's `#k=` page plays on that server (unset: `/ws` on the page's own origin). `assets/_headers` and `assets/404.html` are in every build (`publicDir` is `assets`).

- **Cloudflare Pages** (verified): `assets/_headers` (`/*` then the two header lines above, indented two spaces); `wrangler pages deploy dist --project-name <name>` from `games/reference/` (create the project once with `--force`, or wrangler delegates to Workers static assets). Headers also on a `304`.
- **Netlify** (unverified, from its docs): the same `_headers` file, or `netlify.toml` with `[[headers]] for = "/*"` and `[headers.values]` holding the two headers. Not applied to proxied content or functions.
- **Vercel** (unverified, from its docs): `vercel.json` with `"headers": [{ "source": "/(.*)", "headers": [` the two key/value pairs `] }]`.
- **GitHub Pages:** cannot set headers; `coi-serviceworker` is a workaround this repo has not tested.
