## Goal
Per client, chunk data is paced by a token bucket and sent visible-first, tick frames are held under a soft cap by degrading that client's frame rate, actions and camera reports are rate limited, and the netcode suite asserts deterministic byte counters against `budgets.json`. The 128-chunk cap is measured at full zoom-out and the result is written down.

## Scope
- **Chunk-data bucket** per client: refill and burst from `WorldConfig.bandwidth` (0009; defaults 0010), refilled per tick in integer bytes (rate / tick rate), so it is a function of tick count, not wall time. It gates ChunkEnterPristine, ChunkSnapshots and resync snapshots only; every other section bypasses it (0010: tick frames never queue behind chunk data).
- **Priority** of the per-client enter queue: visible, then distance to `camera + velocity × 0.5 s` (0010 Bandwidth table). A queued chunk that leaves the subscription set is dropped from the queue; a chunk's deltas start only after its enter is sent (0011).
- **Soft cap + degrade** per client: a 1 s sliding byte window over non-chunk sections and the backlog `tick − last_received_tick`; levels 1 → 2 → 4 ticks per message (0010 Rates: Degrade), frames concatenated whole in one message and applied one by one by `on_frame`. A chunk whose queued deltas outgrow its snapshot is sent as a snapshot through the bucket.
- **Heartbeat under degrade:** M28's heartbeat still meets its interval at level 4; asserted.
- **Action rate limit** per connection from `WorldConfig.actionRate` (0004): over-limit actions get `Rejected(Engine(RateLimited))`, unlogged. **Camera-report drop rule** of 0010.
- **Counters → budgets.** `NetCounters` extended (below); ceilings and exact values in `packages/engine/budgets.json` under `net.*`.
- **New fixture `fixtures/busy-field`:** a dense field of timer-driven entities doing whole-value puts at the rate of 0010's "200 active machines" worked number, plus a bench-only genesis that fills chunks to the "dense chunk" figure.
- **Churn measurement** (risk 3): scenario family `zoomout/*`, results recorded in Deviations and as ceilings.

- **Real frame sizes against the bandwidth budget (PRE-PLAN §10).** Three owners, as M15's brief already states: M15 landed counters and fixture ceilings; this brief asserts the 0010 rows under pacing with `busy-field`; **M36b** measures the reference game's busy furnace field and takes the byte-diffing decision of 0011. A miss is answered by byte diffing, never by raising a budget row.
- **The number this milestone has to pace against.** M15 measured `encode_chunk_snapshot`'s O(all entities)-twice-per-chunk cost under a realistic join — 2,000 entities across a 121-chunk view, one connection joining at once: **10.3 ms, 11,131 bytes**. That single join exceeds 0010's entire 10 ms tick-CPU budget on its own, before any other connection's frame or `apply`/`tick` work (`docs/plan/15-connection-and-subscriptions.md`'s Deviations, "Measured, not fixed"). This is what the enter queue and per-tick chunk-bucket refill must pace across ticks rather than pay in one.
- **`bufferedAmount` is ignored in v1.** One backpressure path (`last_received_tick`) on every host, since workerd has none (0009).
- **Degrade recovery** (unstated in 0010): step down one level after 2 s with the window under 75 % of the soft cap and backlog ≤ 2 ticks. Hysteresis avoids oscillation; numbers are config defaults, not Requirements.
- **The hard ceiling is structural,** soft cap + bucket refill; no third limiter. `net.hardCeilingBytesPerS` is asserted over every scenario's worst 1 s window.
- **Frames must be self-delimiting** to concatenate. If M14's framing is not, add an end-of-frame marker here with golden bytes.
- **Risk 3 decision rule.** Trigger: at the maximum view in the dense fixture, p95 `lateVisibleTicks` > 10 at one view-width/s, or `reenterBytes` during a < 64-tile oscillating pan above 25 % of bucket refill. If triggered, open PRE-PLAN §11 item 8 with the numbers; recommended default: cap 144 (ring 1 of the maximum view plus one entering and one retained column), after re-checking the client arena row of 0015 §5. Cap and zoom are Requirement numbers: nothing changes without Tyler. Not triggered: record the numbers, close the risk.

- **Frames are not self-delimiting; no end-of-frame marker.** A marker would change every frame's bytes (the 10-byte heartbeat golden included), and a changed golden is the orchestrator's call. Instead a bundle length-prefixes its frames: `[0x06][n varint]` then `n` x `[len varint][whole frame]`; a lone frame is byte-identical to before. New golden `tests/golden/wire_frame_bundle.hex` (`06020d0100090000000100000002 01070a01000a00000001000000`, written with `pnpm golden:bytes`). No existing golden changed. `ClientCore::on_frame` accepts a `FrameBundle`: validates every frame first, then applies them one by one; returns the last frame's summary.
- **Pacing behaviour.** `build_frame` no longer sends what `subs.entered()` says: enters join a per-connection queue sorted (visible, distance from `camera + velocity / 2` tiles, then (cy, cx)); each tick the bucket (refilled `refill / hz` bytes per elapsed tick, remainder carried, capped at the burst) pays in strict order. A chunk costs its standalone snapshot bytes (`encode_chunk_snapshot` through a `CountSink`; coordinate deltas excluded, at most ~4 B a chunk) or 3 B if pristine; one frame may spend at most half of `out.len()` (32 KiB of the 64 KiB Tx region, so the 128 KB burst spreads over ~4 ticks); a chunk dearer than the whole burst goes out when the bucket is full. A queued chunk that leaves the subscription (cap eviction, unsubscribe) is dropped unsent; deltas, presence-independent, start only for `held` chunks; `region_hash` hashes held chunks, not subscribed ones. Resume `keep` chunks are held at once at no cost.
- **Client change.** A `ChunkSnapshots` entry for a chunk the replica already holds now drops entities overlapping it that the snapshot does not name (`Replica::drop_unnamed_entities`), since a collapsed chunk's `Gone` deltas were never sent.
- **Degrade.** Soft cap: 1 s window (`hz` slots) of frame bytes minus the chunk data the bucket paid for. Level 2 when the window exceeds the cap or the effective backlog exceeds 12 ticks (600 ms, above a p99 RTT of 400 ms); level 4 above 24 ticks or 2 s sustained over the cap; escalates at once, steps down one level after 2 s under 75 % of the cap with backlog at most `2 + (level - 1)` ticks (holding frames itself adds `level - 1` ticks of backlog; without that a degraded client could never read calm). Effective backlog = the last uplink's `now - last_received_tick`, plus silence beyond 22 ticks (uplinks come at least once a second); a stalled client says nothing, so silence has to count, and the link declares a peer dead after 3 s, hence the tight thresholds (a 2.6 s stall reaches level 4 at ~2.35 s). A connection that has never sent a non-zero `last_received_tick` has no backlog (a bare host driver, or a client with no frame yet): `no_alloc_connection` never uplinks and would otherwise degrade. Frames are built every tick and held; one message goes out when `level` ticks have passed since the oldest held frame, or a heartbeat is due (500 ms since the last message, flushing the hold), or the hold reaches half the Tx buffer. A pure heartbeat is not added to a non-empty hold. `bytes_down` counts emitted bytes.
- **Single-player is unpaced.** `SimConfig.unpaced` (`BandwidthConfig::unpaced`: no bucket limit, no degrade) is set by `createClient`'s local host via `buildSimInstanceConfig(cfg, { unpaced: true })`: the stepped test entries advance the host far faster than the client uplinks, which read as a stalled peer and held the last frames back (`reference_depletion_visible` failed until this). `createWorldServer` (netcode, servers) stays paced.
- **Delta collapse.** Per held chunk the host tallies estimated delta bytes since the last message (tile 6 B, entity put `2 +` its encoded size, attributed to the anchor chunk); past `max(64, snapshot bytes)` it stops sending that chunk's deltas, marks it collapsed and queues it as a snapshot through the bucket. Applies at every level (at level 1 the tally is one tick). `rates/deltas-collapse-to-snapshot`: 30 adjacent tile writes collapse one chunk once (snapshot 133 B, deltas 33 B).
- **Counters (host side).** Read through a real export, `sim_pacing_counters(conn) -> status` (`ABI_VERSION` 33 -> 34, `Instance::sim_pacing_counters`, row in `ABI_EXPORTS`; the coordinator overruled my first cut, a top-bit page on `sim_conn_counters`): fourteen LE `u32`s (`reentersWithin5s, reenterBytes, capEvictions, lateVisibleTicksMax, lateVisibleTicksP95, degradeLevel, degradedTicks, queuedEnters, bucketTokens, heldChunks, collapses, bundles, maxEmitGap, orderViolations`). `NetHarnessCounters` carries all of them (`harness.counters(i)`); `worstSecondBytesDown`, sections and frames now parse `FrameBundle`s. `lateVisibleTicks` counts from the tick the host first saw the chunk visible while queued. `orderViolations` (a chunk sent ahead of a still-queued visible one) reads 0 and is asserted.
