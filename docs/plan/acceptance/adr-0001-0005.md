Audit of `docs/decisions/0001` to `0005`, each decision taken as amended (0001 by 0024 §6; 0003 by 0023, 0024 §7/§9, 0035/0042, 0046; 0004 by 0023, 0024 §8; 0005 by 0024 §2/§3/§4/§8, 0038). Format: `docs/plan/acceptance/README.md`. Test suites: `rust` (cargo, including `fixtures/` and `games/reference/sim`), `unit` (`src/**/*.test.ts`), `wasm` (`tests/wasm`), `netcode` (`tests/netcode`, `games/reference/tests/netcode`), `browser` (Playwright specs). Rust doc-tests run in the `doctests` step of `scripts/suites.mjs`, not under a suite name; the one cited is named in its guard text.
