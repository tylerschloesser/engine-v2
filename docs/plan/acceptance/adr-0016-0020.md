Audit unit for [0016](../../decisions/0016-zero-gc-definition.md) (zero GC), [0017](../../decisions/0017-packaging-and-build.md) (packaging and build), [0018](../../decisions/0018-renderer.md) (renderer), [0019](../../decisions/0019-camera-input-and-overlay.md) (camera, input, overlay) and [0020](../../decisions/0020-testing-strategy.md) (testing strategy). Decisions are audited as amended: 0016 by 0024 §12, 0026, 0027 (superseded by 0028), 0028, 0029, 0039, 0043; 0017 by 0024 §13, 0034 (closed by 0045), 0044, 0045, 0050; 0018 by 0024 §9 and §11; 0019 by 0024 §7; 0020 by 0024 §14, 0031, 0033, 0036, 0047, 0048, 0049. Zero-GC test titles are generated by `zeroGcSuite` (`packages/engine/tests/browser/gc/suite.ts`): `<pageId> clean`, `<pageId> neg object|burst <isolate>` (`burst` and, on `reference_single_player`, worker `object` are `@slow`), `<pageId> neg post-message main<->sim` (`gc-loop` only).
