# M39r: M18-pick lands on the Pixel, and a tap's target is recorded

Status: not started · After: 39q · Tyler-dependent: no

## Goal
Finding 7 of the driven rounds: M18-pick passes on the iPhone (9 rings, 0 misses) and fails on the Pixel 5. In every Android round the act "finishes" in 0.6 s, five minutes of silence follow, and the result is a fail with `rings_tapped 0`. The evidence JSON holds only `{ready, actTimedOut: true}`: no tap coordinates, no pick results, no event targets. A read-only diagnosis (2026-10-06) cleared the driver's coordinate mapping (`drive/android.mjs` `toScreen`, `adb shell input tap`, calibrated by `learnChin`; the same mapping serves tap-tile and M18-touch-ghost) and the engine's pick (`input/pick.ts`). The likely cause, **unproven** because no round logged where the events landed: at 40 tiles across a 392 px viewport a ring is about 12 px across, and its 14 px button sits directly against it (`RING_BUTTON_CSS` in the device page, `overlay/anchors.ts`). Chrome Android's touch adjustment snaps a tap near a `<button>` onto the button; WebKit doesn't. The driver's nudge (`person.mjs`, 4 px under the button) is inside the snap radius. The only Pixel probe (39j Deviations) fits: 11 px under the anchor picks the ring, 9 px hits the button.

When this is done the evidence says where each tap landed, the cause is measured, and M18-pick passes on the Pixel without hiding a real-finger problem.

## Order of work
1. **Record targets.** The M18 collector or the device page logs capture-phase `pointerdown`, `pointerup` and `click` (target tag or id, `clientX`/`clientY`, `pointerType`) per tap into the evidence. On an act timeout it writes the last events seen instead of only `actTimedOut`. Unit-test the evidence shape.
2. **Measure.** Don't drive the phone yourself. Give the orchestrator one command (`pnpm device:walk --auto --drive android --tunnel --round m39r-probe --only M18-pick`) and stop with a report. The orchestrator runs it and sends you the evidence. The diagnosis's hypothesis stands or falls on whether the `pointerdown` target is the button.
3. **Fix at the cause.** If it is touch adjustment: in the fixture page, give each ring's button at least 24 px clearance from its ring at every zoom the item uses. Keep the ring its real size, and don't use `pointer-events: none` during the ring phase (that hides what a real finger meets). Make the driver's `tap-ring` choose the point inside the ring with the most clearance from every button box. Below about 12 px clearance it reports `NotDrivable: ring too close to a button at this zoom` at once instead of hanging for five minutes. If step 2 shows something else, stop and report.
4. **Tests**: a unit test that the chosen tap point keeps at least the clearance from every button box (red on today's `bottom + 4` rule at 9.8 px per tile), and one that a too-tight layout yields `NotDrivable` with no wait.

## Non-scope

## Deviations
**Step 1 (targets).** `collect-touch.js`: `K.touch.tapLog()` (capture-phase `pointerdown`/`pointerup`/`click` into 24 preallocated slots written in place; `since(n)` copies once per tap), `K.touch.nearestButton`, `viewInfo()`. Each `pick.taps[]` entry is `{zoom, expected, got, events:[{type,tag,id,x,y,sx,sy,pt,t}], at:{id,zoom,x,y,button:{left,top,right,bottom,gapPx},view:{inner,outer,dpr,vv,scroll}}}`. An act timeout returns `{ready, actTimedOut, tapLog:{asked, events (last 24), sinceAsk, tapsDone}, errors}`. `fake-agent-page.mjs` gained `fire(name, ev)`, `o.check`, `o.buttons`.

**Step 2 (probe, Pixel 5, `m39r-probe`, tunnel).** M18-pick failed by act timeout. `tapLog.asked` = ring 16, zoom 40, point (196.52, 345.45), nearest button box left 189.38 top 326.64 right 203.38 bottom 340.64 (4.8 px from the point). Events: `pointerdown BUTTON (201.45, 349.45) touch`, `pointerup BUTTON` same point, `click BUTTON (197, 340)`. **Cause: Chrome Android's touch adjustment retargets the `pointerdown` itself to the `<button>` from 8.8 px below its edge** (the click point is clamped into the button's box); the engine's canvas never sees the tap. The hypothesis stands. Engine pick and overlay are not implicated.

**Landing offset (+4.9, +4.0) CSS px: not explained, not a rounding error.** The driver aimed at (196.38, 344.64) (button centre x, bottom + 4), the pointerdown arrived at (201.45, 349.45): (+5.07, +4.81). `toScreen` rounding is at most 0.5/dpr = 0.18 CSS px (unit test in `device-walk-ringtap.test.mjs`). The 39j measurement had every hit within 1 px at four points, and `learnChin` learns only y (x is `outerWidth - innerWidth - rightInset`, uncalibrated; it logs the x it saw but does not use it). The 1/2.75 fractions in the pointer position say the raw physical pixel (554, 961) was delivered, so the driver's placement (or the page's `outer`/`inner` at tap time) is a suspect; Chrome moving the pointer coordinates is not excluded. No driver change made, since the evidence cannot separate them. For the next round: each tap now records `sx, sy` (screenX/Y) and `view` (inner, outer, dpr, visualViewport offset and scale, scroll), and `android.mjs` `tap` logs `css -> screen px, page offset, dpr`. With the buttons gone from the ring's reach, the next Pixel round shows whether pointerdown lands at the asked point. A 5 px error would still miss a 12 px ring at 40 tiles.

**Step 3 (fix).** The brief's "button at least 24 px from its ring at every zoom" cannot hold with all 50 buttons mounted: at 40 tiles across 392 px the rings sit 29 px apart, so any lifted button lands on a neighbouring ring or within about 15 px of one (best point of a shifted lattice: about 15 px from a ring edge, before the 14 px box). Done instead (decision for the orchestrator, see report): `device.ts` `ringPhase({pickId})` mounts only the target ring's button (the rest `display: none`, never `pointer-events: none`) and lifts it by `RING_BUTTON_CLEAR_PX` (26) plus the ring's top edge (0.35 tile) with a `--ring-clear` margin; `ringPhase()` ends it. `pick()` runs the phase per tap; the button step and the anchors check see the buttons as before (`--ring-clear` is 0 outside the phase). Measured in the headless walk (`walk-touch`, evidence `pick.taps[].at.button.gapPx - 0.6 tile`): ring edge to button box 26.0 px at 40, 20 and 12 tiles, 9 taps, 0 misses, `buttonChanged` false. `person.mjs`: `chooseRingTap(ring, boxes)` (point inside 0.85 r with the most clearance from every visible button box) and `MIN_RING_CLEARANCE_PX = 12` (`NotDrivable: ring too close to a button at this zoom (...)`, no tap). The ring's pick radius reaches the driver as `#walk-ring`'s `data-r`.

**Existing test changed (flag).** `device-walk-drive.test.mjs` "a ring under its own button is tapped just below the button, the button itself at its centre" asserted the replaced `bottom + 4` rule; its ring half now asserts the far-side point (the button half is unchanged). Renamed accordingly.

**Step 4.** `scripts/lib/device-walk-ringtap.test.mjs` (4 + 1 tests: NotDrivable with no tap on today's 4.8 px layout, the point inside the ring 12 px from every box, most-clearance choice, the button step unchanged, `toScreen` rounding), `device-walk-tapring.test.mjs` (3 tests). **Round `m39r-pixel` (Pixel 5, tunnel): touch adjustment fixed, two driver defects.** Every pointerdown targeted CANVAS (8 of 9 taps); the act timed out on the 9th.
1. **x offset: a driver defect, fixed.** `screenY - clientY` was 105.45 on every tap, equal to the driver's `offY` (851 - 745 - 0.5), so y placement is right (taps from y 253 to 653 landed on the aim). x was +5.07 css px on every tap (probe and this round): the driver's `offX` is the formula `outerWidth - innerWidth - rightInset` = 393 - 392 - 0 = 1, and the raw physical pixel delivered (554 = 201.45 x 2.75) says the page's true x offset on this phone is -4.0 css px. `learnChin` read the calibration tap's x and never used it. Now `android.mjs` `calibrationFrom(m, hit, px, py)` returns `{chin, offX, xBias}`; `xBias` (measured offset minus the formula's) is stored per page kind and `offsetsFor(m, chin, xBias)` adds it, so the other orientation keeps its formula (a cutout). Tests in `device-walk-ringtap.test.mjs` with inner [392,745], outer [393,851], dpr 2.75: seen red (`expected 5.07 to be less than 0.2`), the formula alone lands 4 px right. The first Pixel tap of a round is still placed with the formula for the calibration tap only (that tap is swallowed).
2. **The 9th tap on a DIV: the walk bar (`id walk-bar`), not an offset.** Ring 36 at 12 tiles: the pick radius there is 0.6 x 745/12 = 37 px (the long side per tile is 62 px; I had pictured the width), the driver took the point farthest from the lifted button, 0.85 r = 31 px below the ring centre, y 649 asked, 653.5 landed (the +4 is the sending offset's y rounding and the x defect's twin; `sy - cy` shows no y error), and the bar covers the bottom of the page while it asks. Fixes: the collector asks only for rings whose centre has the canvas under `elementFromPoint` and whose tappable disc ends at least `BAR_RESERVE_PX` (170) above the bottom; skipped ones are in `pick.skipped[{id, zoom, under}]`. The driver reads `#walk-bar`'s rect and `chooseRingTap(ring, boxes, minClear, covers)` keeps every point 8 px clear of it, `NotDrivable: ring is under the walk bar or another element at this zoom` when none is. Tests (`device-walk-ringtap.test.mjs`, `device-walk-tapring.test.mjs`) seen red with both guards disabled (`5.55 to be >= 8`, `expected 'done' to be 'notDrivable'`, the skipped list). `fake-agent-page.mjs` gained `elementAt`.
